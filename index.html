AI for Data & Analysis

Today you will read data with AI, describe trends in clear English, and check every AI number with a spreadsheet formula.

  1. Describe a trend with at least two trend phrases.
  2. Choose a line, bar or pie chart and say why.
  3. Check three AI numbers with formulas and find the wrong ones.
  4. Find three checked insights and one limitation, and show them in one chart.
  5. Decide in a group if a chart is honest or misleading.

How to read a formula: B2 = column B, row 2 (online sales in January). B2:B13 = all the cells from B2 to B13 (January to December).
=SUM(…) adds them · =AVERAGE(…) finds the average · =MAX(…) finds the highest number. When you click Calculate, the cells in the formula turn gold.
